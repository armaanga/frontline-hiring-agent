How to navigate the Frontline Hiring Agent prototypes

There are two prototypes. One shows what recruiters, admins and auditors see inside the HR platform; the other shows what candidates see on their phone. All names and data are made up, and dates are set around 24–31 Oct 2026. Best viewed in a desktop browser.

1Platform: recruiter, admin and auditor view

Left menu: the platform's normal pages (Home, Requisitions…) are placeholders. The new pages are under ✦ AI Screening Agent. Use View as (top right) to switch between Recruiter, Admin and Auditor (auditors can't edit settings).

OverviewLast-7-day numbers, anything waiting for approval, and whether the agent is on in each country.
Agent runsEvery time the agent processed new applications, per job and day. Open one to see checks, ranking, invites and messages.
SettingsHow often the agent runs, how many to shortlist per job, reserve list, approvals per country, messaging rules, safety limits.
Audit trailEvery decision for every applicant, filterable by date, country and job, with exports.

Suggested tour:

  1. See the agent on the existing Applications pageOpen Applications. Three columns marked ✦ are added by the agent (status, score, channel). Switch between the Dallas, Bengaluru and Leipzig job tabs. Click Marcus Vance to open his ✦ Agent activity, the full time-stamped trail.
  2. Review and approve today's shortlistClick Review shortlist (10) → on the green banner. Use − / + to change the shortlist size and watch the cut-off line move. Click anyone below the line, then Add to shortlist; the reason is logged. Click Approve & invite top 10, then open the Invites tab to see bookings and declines.
  3. Change a settingOpen Settings. Try a different run frequency or shortlist size, then click Save changes. Germany's automatic options are locked on purpose.
  4. Audit itOpen Audit trail (or switch View as to Auditor). Filter by date range, country, job or outcome, then click any row to see what the agent checked, how it ranked them, who approved, and what they were told. Check the Agent runs and Settings changes tabs (your saved change appears there), the fairness table at the bottom, and Export CSV / Export (JSON).

2Candidate experience: the applicant's phone

  1. Pick a story on the leftMarcus: no CV, answers 3 questions, gets shortlisted and books an interview. Lukas: can't work the required shift, isn't selected, asks why. Priya: meets the requirements but isn't selected, asks why.
  2. Reply in the phoneTap the reply buttons. The dashed ⏩ Skip ahead to the update button jumps forward in time. You can also type your own message, e.g. "why was I rejected?", "change time" or "talk to a person".
  3. Open "My application"Tap the link in the chat or See my application, then Verify and continue (the code is pre-filled). The page shows the candidate's details and answers. If they weren't selected, there's a Why wasn't I selected? button.
  4. Try SMSSwitch WhatsApp / SMS in the "What happens in this story" card. The checklist there ticks off each step as you go.

What to notice

  • Missing information leads to a question on WhatsApp/SMS, not a rejection.
  • Recruiters set the shortlist size and approve it; Germany always needs a recruiter.
  • Every step is recorded for auditors and can't be edited.
  • Candidates never see scores or rankings, only their own data and a short reason if not selected.
