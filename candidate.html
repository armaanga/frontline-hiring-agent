Recruiter platform ↗Audit trail ↗
Candidate-facing · no app, no login

Applicants talk to the agent on WhatsApp or SMS

The agent asks only what's missing, sends updates and books interviews. Applicants can see their application data through a secure link (no password, just a code to their phone) and ask why they weren't selected. Scores and rankings are never shown to candidates.

What happens in this story

Tap the reply buttons, or type your own message, e.g. why was I rejected? change time talk to a person

Rules the agent follows with candidates

• Fixed, approved questions, and only for information that's missing.
• Anyone not selected can ask why and gets a short, polite reason.
• Candidates see their application data, never scores, rankings, criteria or review timings.
• Quiet hours 21:00–08:00. SMS if WhatsApp isn't available. Local languages.
• Anything it can't answer goes to a recruiter.
